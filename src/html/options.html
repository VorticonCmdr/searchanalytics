Page aggregation

In Property by hour → Pages, URLs that share an ID are combined into one row — useful when the same article is reachable under several URLs (AMP, tracking parameters, slugs).

The first capture group ( … ) becomes the ID. URLs that don’t match keep their full URL. Leave empty to turn aggregation off.

Google Search Status events

Import algorithm updates, core updates and incidents from the Google Search Status Dashboard into your event list. They appear as bands and lines on the Property by day chart.
